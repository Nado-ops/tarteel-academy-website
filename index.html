Our Vision

To be a leading centre of Islamic and holistic education, empowering individuals of all ages through faith, knowledge, skills development and community service, while nurturing excellence and positive impact locally and globally.

◎

Our Mission

Tarteel Academy is committed to providing accessible, high-quality education through its diverse faculties, integrating Qur’anic studies, academic learning, skills development, community upliftment and personal growth. We strive to develop confident, compassionate and capable individuals who contribute meaningfully to society while upholding strong Islamic values.
