# Tarteel Academy Photography Guidelines

## Approved visual direction

Tarteel Academy requires a modest and reserved approach to all live photography used on the website.

Live photographs of male and female learners, staff, volunteers and community members may be used, provided that identifiable faces are avoided wherever reasonably possible.

## Preferred compositions

- Back-facing subjects
- Side angles where the face is not clearly identifiable
- Over-the-shoulder classroom or learning scenes
- Activity-focused photographs showing hands, books, Qur'ans, computers, tools or community work
- Wider group scenes where individuals are not identifiable
- Carefully cropped photographs that exclude or obscure faces naturally

## Avoid

- Front-facing portraits
- Close-up identifiable faces
- Images where responsive cropping exposes a face on desktop, tablet or mobile
- Inappropriate or culturally unsuitable clothing
- Artificial blur placed directly over faces unless approved as a last resort

## Acceptance checks

Before merging or deploying replacement images:

- Confirm no clearly identifiable face is visible at desktop width.
- Confirm no clearly identifiable face becomes visible because of `background-size: cover` or `object-fit: cover` at tablet and mobile widths.
- Confirm image quality is suitable for high-density screens.
- Confirm subjects, clothing and setting are appropriate for Tarteel Academy.
- Confirm alternative text describes the activity without naming or identifying individuals.
- Obtain Tarteel Academy approval of the final image set.

## Deployment gate

Do not merge this photography update into `main` until all five affected locations have approved replacement assets and responsive verification has been completed.
